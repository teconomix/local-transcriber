Fact sheet for lists and articles

Everything you need to list or describe Local Transcriber, in one place: descriptions in three lengths, the key facts, requirements, screenshots and links. All statements refer to the current version.

In one line

Local Transcriber – meetings, calls and dictation transcribed on your Mac, with speaker recognition and no cloud.

In about 50 words

Local Transcriber is a free, open-source menu bar app for Macs with Apple Silicon. It records meetings and calls, transcribes them with Whisper, recognizes who spoke with pyannote, and lets you dictate into any app at the press of a key. Everything runs locally: no account, no subscription, no audio leaving the computer.

In about 120 words

Local Transcriber is a menu bar app for macOS that records conversations, transcribes them and recognizes the speakers – entirely on the Mac. Microphone and system audio are recorded as separate tracks; video calls in Teams, Zoom, Meet and other apps are detected, and the recording stops when the call ends. Speech recognition uses Whisper large-v3-turbo on the Apple Silicon GPU (MLX), speaker separation uses pyannote with a local voice database: name someone once, and they are recognized next time. Holding a key dictates text into any app. Optionally, a local or cloud language model of your choice writes minutes or summaries – cloud only on click, with names replaced by placeholders. The app is free and open source (MIT).

Facts

  • Name: Local Transcriber (“Transcriber” in the menu bar)
  • Category: transcription, speaker recognition, dictation; menu bar app for macOS
  • Price and license: free, open source (MIT); no account, no subscription, no license server
  • Requirements: Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, about 8 GB of free disk space, 16 GB of memory recommended. AI features on the device (dictation cleanup, file names): macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • Languages: German and English (interface and documentation)
  • Technology: Swift/SwiftUI app, Python core; Whisper large-v3-turbo via MLX, pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1, ffmpeg, SQLite
  • Speed: one hour of audio including speaker recognition in about three to four minutes (Apple Silicon GPU)
  • Privacy: audio, transcripts and voice profiles stay on the Mac; internet is only needed for installation and for a cloud model if you choose one
  • Output: plain text or Markdown, JSON with word timestamps, SRT
  • For power users: command line, folder watcher, MCP server for AI agents, plugin for Obsidian
  • Developer: Björn Rabethge (legal notice)

Install

curl -fsSL https://transcriber.teconomix.net/k/press/install.sh | bash

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask teconomix/tap/local-transcriber. In a list entry, the general command without the path /k/press is best: curl -fsSL https://transcriber.teconomix.net/install.sh | bash.
