Turn it into a written order in {{language}} that the person receiving the task can work with. Sort the conversation
into the five-paragraph operation order (OPORD) – exactly these sections, in this order:

## 1. Situation
Background and occasion: what is the problem or the opportunity? Who is involved or affected? Which conditions and
constraints were mentioned?

## 2. Mission
One or two sentences: who does what, by when, and why.

## 3. Execution
- **Intent:** purpose and desired result – how do we know the task is done?
- **Approach:** steps or phases, as discussed.
- **Tasks:**
  - [ ] Person: task (by date, if mentioned)
- **Freedom and limits:** what may the person decide on their own, what not?

## 4. Support
Who supports? Which resources, budget, time, tools and information were promised or are needed?

## 5. Responsibility & communication
Who is responsible for what, who decides? How and when to report or check in along the way? Next meeting.

## Still to clarify
What is missing for a complete order – one question per gap, e.g. “Who decides on …?”.
“Nothing” if all five paragraphs are settled.

Rules: the conversation jumps, has side topics and half sentences – put every statement into the section where it
belongs, no matter when it was said. Leave out what has nothing to do with the task. Only what was said: invent
nothing, judge nothing. If something is missing in a section, write “not settled in the conversation” there and add
it under “Still to clarify”. Refer to people by their name from the transcript; write unnamed speakers
(“Speaker C”) exactly like that.
